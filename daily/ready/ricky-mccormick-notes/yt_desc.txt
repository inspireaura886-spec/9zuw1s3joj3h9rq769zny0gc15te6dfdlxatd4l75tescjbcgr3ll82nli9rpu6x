Two handwritten notes were found in the pockets of Ricky McCormick, 41, whose body was found in a cornfield in St. Charles County, Missouri, on June 30, 1999. The medical examiner ruled the cause of death undetermined. The notes hold more than 30 lines of letters, numbers, dashes and parentheses. The FBI's Cryptanalysis and Racketeering Records Unit and the American Cryptogram Association could not break them, and in March 2011 the FBI asked the public for help; responses poured in and it set up a page just for theories. The unit says it cannot get past step two of breaking a code: working out the system. In 2011 the FBI said his family had told them he used coded notes since he was a boy; in 2012 his family told the Riverfront Times they never knew him to write in code and that Ricky could barely write his own name, and some experts think he may only have carried the notes. The case is still open.
