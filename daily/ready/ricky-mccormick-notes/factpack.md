# Fact pack: ricky-mccormick-notes (AI Mode 2026-11-04, user-approved; Hark fact-check)
Verified facts (use only these):
- Body of Ricky McCormick, 41, found 30 Jun 1999 in a field/cornfield in St. Charles County near St. Louis, Missouri (FBI 2011; CBS 2011; Riverfront Times 2012; Wikipedia; Popular Mechanics 2026).
- Two handwritten encrypted notes were found in his trouser/jeans pockets (FBI; CBS; RFT; FBI Vault lab file).
- More than 30 lines of letters, numbers, dashes and parentheses (FBI); capital letters, numbers, dashes and parentheses (CBS).
- FBI Cryptanalysis and Racketeering Records Unit (CRRU) and the American Cryptogram Association failed to decipher them (FBI; RFT; Wikipedia).
- 29 Mar 2011 the FBI asked the public for help; outpouring of responses; special web page (FBI; CBS; Wikipedia).
- CRRU chief Dan Olson: "We cannot get past step two" (RFT 2012; Popular Mechanics 2026: "the FBI can't get past step two").
- CRRU cracks nearly all ciphers it gets: ~1% of solvable codes go unbroken (RFT); "reportedly cracks 99 percent" (Popular Mechanics).
- Medical examiner ruled the cause of death "undetermined" (RFT; Popular Mechanics; Wikipedia via St. Louis Post-Dispatch).
- Family: "The only thing he could write was his name. He didn't write in no code." (mother, RFT 2012); family remembered him as illiterate (Popular Mechanics). The FBI 2011 page says family told them he had used coded notes since boyhood: the two accounts conflict (that conflict is the story).
- Cipher expert Elonka Dunin: "I don't think McCormick wrote these notes. Perhaps he was a courier." (RFT; Popular Mechanics).
- Case still open; flurry of tips; no arrests (Popular Mechanics 2026; RFT 2012: no arrests).
Dropped / not voiced: his 1993 conviction (true but irrelevant and harmful framing); drug-courier and named suspects from police files (defamation risk, unproven); "15 miles from home / no car" (Wikipedia says 15 mi, RFT/PopMech say 20 mi: conflict); "7,000 comments" (one source); "ranked third on CRRU list" (one source); fingertips/decomposition detail (gore); "FBI says murdered" (ME ruling is 'undetermined', so the script says undetermined).
