On 10 July 1969 the RMV Picardy found the trimaran Teignmouth Electron drifting empty in the Atlantic. Donald Crowhurst's logbooks showed he had never left the Atlantic while radioing false positions in the Golden Globe Race. His last entry ended 'It is the mercy.' He was never found.
