# teignmouth-electron-crowhurst-1969 (verified 2026-11-06)
Hook: A trimaran drifted in mid-Atlantic with the table set for a hero's welcome: the captain's logbooks showed he had never gone round the world.
Facts: Donald Crowhurst, amateur sailor, entered the 1968-69 Sunday Times Golden Globe race, staked his house on it; radioed false positions; found by RMV Picardy 7:50 am 10 Jul 1969, ~700 mi W of Azores, empty. Logbooks recorded real route, a false speed claim, and false navigational workings. From 24 Jun he wrote ~25,000 words of philosophy, ending 1 Jul 'It is finished - it is finished IT IS THE MERCY'. Never found; presumed lost overboard.
Last line: He never reached the finish. His logbook did.
