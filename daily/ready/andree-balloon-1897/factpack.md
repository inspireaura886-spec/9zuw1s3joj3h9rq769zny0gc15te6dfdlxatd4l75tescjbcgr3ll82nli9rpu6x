Andrée's 1897 hydrogen balloon *Örnen* (*Eagle*) was solved in August–September 1930** when Norwegian sealers on Kvitøya discovered the final camp, three bodies, handwritten diaries, and exposed Kodak film (from which 93 photographs were successfully developed, proving the balloon crashed after 2 days and the men trekked south across the pack ice for nearly three months). ### Official / Mainstream Explanation & Why It Is Still Argued
- **Official / Mainstream Explanation & Why It Is Still Argued:** The **33-year disappearance** of Andrée's 1897 balloon expedition is **fully solved**: the August–September 1930 *Bratvaag* and *Isbjørn* discoveries on Kvitøya recovered the camp, diaries, and 93 developed photographs (`F7, F8, F9, F10`), proving the hydrogen balloon *Örnen* lost its drag ropes at launch (`F4`), leaked gas through 8 million stitch holes (`F3`), crashed on the pack ice after 51.5 hours on 14 July 1897 (`F5`), and forced a grueling three-month trek south to Kvitøya (`F9, F11, F12`). What is **still argued** by physicians and polar historians is **what killed the three young/middle-aged men within days of landing on Kvitøya in October 1897** despite having food, fuel, and rifles (`F13, F14, F15`).

### What Is Genuinely Still Unexplained
- **What Is Genuinely Still Unexplained:** Because the three bodies were cremated in Stockholm in October 1930 without a complete modern forensic autopsy (`F13`), researchers still debate whether they died of *Trichinella spiralis* parasites from undercooked polar bear meat (`F14`), hypothermia and exhaustion, carbon monoxide from their Primus stove, or a polar bear attack that killed Strindberg (wedged into a cliff cleft) and Frænkel before Andrée took morphine (`F15`).
