On 13 September 2026 at about 21:13 UTC, U.S. Space Forces-Space confirmed that USA 32 (#19460), a satellite launched in 1988 and identified by analysts as the NRO signals-intelligence satellite FARRAH III, fragmented in low Earth orbit at roughly 775 km. A Space Force spokesperson told Newsweek the cause is unknown. Independent analysts suggest an aged battery or tank rupture or a debris strike; no government has said it was destroyed on purpose.
