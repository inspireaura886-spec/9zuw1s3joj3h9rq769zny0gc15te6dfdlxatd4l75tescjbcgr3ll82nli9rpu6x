### Official / Mainstream Explanation & Why It Is Still Argued
- **Official / Mainstream Explanation & Why It Is Still Argued:** U.S. Space Forces-Space (`S4S`) officially confirmed the fragmentation of `USA 32` (`#19460`) at ~21:13 UTC on 13 September 2026 (`F1, F2`), but neither the Space Force nor the National Reconnaissance Office has released a cause or fragment count (`F2, F16`). Independent space-domain analysts at Space Weather, Italy's INAF, and OKAPI:Orbits argue the leading hypotheses are accidental—either an internal rupture of a 38-year-old unpassivated battery/propellant tank (`F8, F9, F10`) or a hypervelocity strike by small untracked debris inside the congested 775–790 km polar shell (`F9, F11, F12`)—rather than viral speculation linking it to the Air Force's 15 September 2026 disclosure of 'on-orbit space control weapons' (`F8`).

### What Is Genuinely Still Unexplained
- **What Is Genuinely Still Unexplained:** Because `USA 32` is a classified 1988 NRO payload with no public design blueprint or official fragment count (`F2, F16`), analysts still do not know whether an internal battery explosion or an external 11 km/s debris impact triggered the 13 September 2026 breakup (`F8, F9, F10`), nor whether the five objects optically tracked near the main remnant are its three dish antennas and two solar panels (`F13, F14`).
