### Official / Mainstream Explanation & Why It Is Still Argued
- **Official / Mainstream Explanation & Why It Is Still Argued:** When the FBI closed its 17-year investigation on 31 December 1979, it officially concluded that Frank Morris, John Anglin, and Clarence Anglin most likely drowned in the frigid waters and strong currents of San Francisco Bay after their raincoat raft broke up (`F8, F15, F16`), noting that personal family photos and life vests washed up and no verified thefts occurred on shore (`F8, F15`). It remains argued because no bodies of the three men ever washed up (`F8, F11`), inmate John Paul Scott swam from Alcatraz to Fort Point six months later in December 1962 (`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_1962_Alcatraz_escape`), Delft University's 2014 hydraulic model proved an 11:30 p.m. launch could reach Horseshoe Bay while sending debris toward Angel Island (`F9, F10`), and FBI tests on the 2013 'John Anglin' letter were inconclusive (`F11, F12, F13`).

### What Is Genuinely Still Unexplained
- **What Is Genuinely Still Unexplained:** Sixty-four years after the breakout on 11 June 1962, investigators have never found the bodies of Frank Morris, John Anglin, or Clarence Anglin, nor definitively proven whether the trio drowned in San Francisco Bay or reached the Marin County shore (`F1, F2, F9, F10, F13, F15, F16`).
