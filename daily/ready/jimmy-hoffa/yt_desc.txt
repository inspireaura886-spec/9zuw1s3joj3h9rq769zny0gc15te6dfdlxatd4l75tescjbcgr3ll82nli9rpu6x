Former Teamsters president Jimmy Hoffa was last seen on July 30, 1975, in the parking lot of the Machus Red Fox restaurant in Bloomfield Township, Michigan, where he was due to meet two reputed organized-crime figures. His car was found in the lot. He was declared legally dead in 1982, but his body has never been found. The FBI dug up a field in Oakland Township in 2013, and in 2021-22, acting on a tip, searched land under the Pulaski Skyway in Jersey City; nothing of evidentiary value was found. No one has been arrested. On the 50th anniversary in 2025, FBI Detroit said the investigation remains active.
