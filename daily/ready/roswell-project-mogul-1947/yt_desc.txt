On July 8, 1947, the 509th Bomb Group at Roswell Army Air Field in New Mexico issued an official press release announcing it had recovered a 'flying disc' from a ranch near Corona—only for General Roger Ramey to announce the next day that the tinfoil, rubber, and balsa sticks came from a weather balloon. After decades of UFO cover-up claims, a 1994 U.S. Air Force report concluded the material was most likely from Project Mogul, a then-top-secret balloon project launched from Alamogordo to monitor Soviet atomic bomb tests; a 1997 Air Force report addressed the 'alien bodies' stories.

Was it a flying saucer, or a secret the Army hid behind a weather balloon?
