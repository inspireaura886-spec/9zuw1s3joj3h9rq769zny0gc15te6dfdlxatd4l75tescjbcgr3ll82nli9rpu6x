On 22 September 1979 a US Vela satellite recorded a double flash over the ocean near the Prince Edward Islands, the signature of a nuclear blast. A White House panel said probably not a bomb; Navy hydrophone data and iodine-131 reported in Australian sheep pointed the other way. No country has ever admitted it.
