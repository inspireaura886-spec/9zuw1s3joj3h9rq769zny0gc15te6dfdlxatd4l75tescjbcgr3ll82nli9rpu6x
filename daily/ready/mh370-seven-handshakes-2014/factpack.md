# Fact pack: mh370-seven-handshakes-2014 (Gemini VIRAL-PICK GB-20261104-2100 #1; user pick 2026-11-05 09:52 IST; Hark fact-check 2026-11-05 IST)
Verified facts (all from fetched pages):
- 8 Mar 2014, Malaysia Airlines MH370, Boeing 777-200ER 9M-MRO, Kuala Lumpur -> Beijing; 239 aboard = 12 crew + 227 passengers (ATSB AE-2014-054 p.~10; Wikipedia). Cause not determined (Wikipedia lead; ATSB: "reasons for the loss of MH370 cannot be established with certainty until the aircraft is found").
- Last radio call 01:19 MYT: "Good night Malaysian three seven zero" (ATSB: PIC acknowledged at 1719:30 UTC; Wikipedia transcript). Script says "the cockpit radioed" (no pilot focus).
- 01:21 MYT lost from ATC secondary radar; transponder data stopped after 01:21 (Wikipedia; ATSB "Waypoint IGARI to MEKAR").
- Military/civil primary radar: from IGARI a 40 deg right turn then a 180 deg left turn "to track almost directly back across the Malay Peninsula" (ATSB); "tracked by the Malaysian military's primary radar system for another hour as it deviated westward" (Wikipedia). Last military radar 02:22 MYT, 200 nm WNW of Penang/Butterworth (Wikipedia radar caption).
- Satellite: aircraft SDU logged on via Inmarsat I-3 Indian Ocean Region satellite to the ground station in Perth (ATSB). Seven handshakes: 02:25 (aircraft log-on), 03:41, 04:41, 05:41, 06:41, 08:10 (ground-initiated), 08:19 (aircraft log-on, "partial"); no reply to 09:15 ping; plus two unanswered satphone calls (Wikipedia SDU log; ATSB handshake table). Intervals ~hourly but not exactly (script avoids "hourly").
- BTO gives only the distance satellite-aircraft; "a series of seven rings" (ATSB). Last transmission 08:19:37 MYT (Wikipedia).
- ATSB: 7th arc ~ fuel exhaustion; "probably impacted the ocean relatively close to the time this last transmission was made". Malaysia (24 Mar 2014): "Flight MH370 ended in the southern Indian Ocean" (Wikipedia). Script only says the final ring crossed the southern Indian Ocean.
- 29 Jul 2015 flaperon (a wing control surface) found on La Reunion (ATSB "The flaperon"); confirmed as MH370 (Wikipedia, debris confirmed).
- Underwater sonar search >120,000 km2, suspended Jan 2017 (ATSB; Wikipedia). Ocean Infinity 2018: ~112,000 km2, nothing (Wikipedia; search excerpts). 2025-26 phases (Mar 2025; 31 Dec 2025-23 Jan 2026) found nothing (Wikipedia 2025-2026 search; The Star 2026-06-30 ~7,500 km2 scanned).
- 29 Jun 2026: Malaysia extended the Ocean Infinity "no find, no fee" deal 1 Jul 2026-30 Jun 2027, US$70M only if found, remaining 7,428.54 km2; assets can be scheduled only Nov 2026-Apr 2027 (calm-sea season) (MOT Malaysia statement PDF; The Star 2026-06-30).
Dropped / not voiced: who did it / pilot theories (unproven, families alive); "crash" stated as certain (only "final ring crossed the southern Indian Ocean"); "hourly" handshakes (not exact); "pinged seven times" by the jet (2 of 7 were aircraft-initiated; script says "kept answering ... seven times" - all 7 are exchanges); 23 Mar 2014 satellite-imagery objects (F2: not voiced); 15,000 km2 figure (not voiced); search "resumed now" (not confirmed - only the window is open); passenger nationalities and all victim detail.
