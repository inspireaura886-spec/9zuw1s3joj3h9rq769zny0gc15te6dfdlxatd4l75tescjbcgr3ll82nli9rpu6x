### Official / Mainstream Explanation & Why It Is Still Argued
- **Official / Mainstream Explanation & Why It Is Still Argued:** The U.S. Department of War and AARO officially list intelligence report `DOW-UAP-D101` and its six videos (`DOW-UAP-PR117`–`PR122`) as **unresolved** (`F2, F4, F13, F14`). Optical analysts note that because the released clips are cellphone recordings of a cabin screen lacking slant-range telemetry (`F11`), nearby cool objects over a lit flare (such as seabirds, spray, or flare debris) can create a parallax illusion of high speed (`F11, F12`). It remains argued because the Special Operations crew logged 25 instances moving in formations and reported two orbs hovering over the flare that bolted horizontally without changing altitude the instant the AC-130J fired its main cannon (`F1, F6, F7, F8, F9`).

### What Is Genuinely Still Unexplained
- **What Is Genuinely Still Unexplained:** Without the underlying raw sensor and slant-range telemetry from the AC-130J gunship (`F11, F13, F14`), investigators cannot conclusively prove whether the ~25 'cold orbs' logged on 8 September 2021 were nearby marine birds/debris startled by cannon fire or genuine fast-moving craft operating over the Gulf of Oman (`F1, F7, F8, F13`).
