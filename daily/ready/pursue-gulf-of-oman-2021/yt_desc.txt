On September 8, 2021, U.S. Special Operations Forces aboard an AC-130J gunship conducting a nighttime live-fire exercise over the Gulf of Oman logged approximately 25 instances of unidentified anomalous phenomena. Released on August 7, 2026 in the fifth batch of the Department of War's PURSUE archive (report DOW-UAP-D101 and video files DOW-UAP-PR117 through DOW-UAP-PR122), the file describes four-foot-diameter 'cold orbs' hovering 20 feet above a target flare and maneuvering in formations between 250 and 1,300 mph. About 15 seconds after the flare was dropped, a crew member fired the aircraft's cannon, and right after that the crew saw the two objects fly quickly away from the flare; the report does not say the firing caused this. The crew said the recorder failed and the video data became corrupted. The Pentagon's PURSUE release describes the case as unresolved.
