### Official / Mainstream Explanation & Why It Is Still Argued
- **Official / Mainstream Explanation & Why It Is Still Argued:** For the **Taraia Object**, the mystery is **officially solved**: on 18–19 October 2026, Purdue University and the Archaeological Legacy Institute announced that close-up inspection of the Nikumaroro lagoon anomaly proved it was a clump of large fallen trees coated in a shiny carbonate coral-sand veneer that mimicked gleaming aluminum in satellite photos (`F8, F9, F10`). For **Amelia Earhart's 1937 disappearance**, the U.S. Navy and most mainstream historians hold the 'crash and sink' explanation that her Lockheed Model 10-E Electra ran out of fuel near Howland Island (`F15, F16`), whereas castaway-hypothesis proponents cite post-loss radio bearings, 1940 bone measurements, and the newly found 30-inch steel tube on Nikumaroro (`F5, F11, F16`).

### What Is Genuinely Still Unexplained
- **What Is Genuinely Still Unexplained:** Despite 89 years of searches—including Robert Ballard's 2019 deep-reef survey (`F14`), Deep Sea Vision's 2024 sonar rock (`F12`), and the October 2026 Purdue/ALI lagoon expedition (`F8, F9`)—not a single verified piece of Earhart's Lockheed Electra `NR16020` has ever been found, and the origin of the 30-inch steel tube pulled from Nikumaroro's lagoon in October 2026 awaits laboratory analysis (`F11, F15, F16`).
