A shape in satellite images of Nikumaroro's lagoon, the "Taraia Object," was first noticed in 2020 and later seen in 1938 aerial photos. A 17-person expedition (Archaeological Legacy Institute, Purdue Research Foundation, Purdue University) investigated in October 2026 and announced on 19 October (local time) that it found no conclusive evidence of Amelia Earhart's Electra: the anomaly was likely a collection of large trees on the sandspit, with a shiny carbonate coating. A steel tube about 30 inches long is still being studied. Earhart's disappearance in July 1937 remains unsolved.
