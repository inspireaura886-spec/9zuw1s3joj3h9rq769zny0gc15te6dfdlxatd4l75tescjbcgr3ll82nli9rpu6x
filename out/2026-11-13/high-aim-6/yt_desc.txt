In January 2003, a Taiwanese long-line fishing ship was found drifting in Australian waters about 150 km east of Rowley Shoals: engine dead, rudder locked, plenty of fuel and food aboard, three tonnes of rotting tuna in the hold, and not one crew member. Days later, calls were still being made in Indonesia from the missing engineer's phone. Indonesian police traced one crewman, who said the captain and engineer had been killed - he never explained why, and no trace of the crew was ever found.
