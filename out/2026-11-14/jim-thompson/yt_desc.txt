On Easter Sunday, 26 March 1967, the American businessman Jim Thompson - the "Thai Silk King" - walked out of Moonlight Cottage in Malaysia's Cameron Highlands after lunch and was never seen again. A manhunt on an unprecedented scale, with British troops and hundreds of volunteers, found no body and no trace. Theories from kidnapping to a staged disappearance have never produced an answer. What happened to Jim Thompson on that afternoon walk?
